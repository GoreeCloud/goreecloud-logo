# GoreeCloud — Official Logo and Visual Identity

## Document Metadata

- **Status:** Approved
- **Approved:** August 19, 2026
- **Document type:** Brand identity and artwork record
- **Authoritative repository:** `GoreeCloud/goreecloud-logo`
- **Canonical artwork:** `official/goreecloud-logo.svg`
- **Identity:** GoreeCloud platform / ecosystem
- **Design direction:** Unified Clean

## Purpose

I use this record to document the approved GoreeCloud platform logo, its meaning, canonical geometry, approved color direction, and usage boundary.

The GoreeCloud platform logo identifies GoreeCloud as a whole. It is not a generic application icon and must not replace the unique canonical identities required for individual GoreeCloud applications and services.

## Approval Decision

I approved the **Unified Clean** direction as the official GoreeCloud logo on August 19, 2026. I also approved blue as the official color direction for this mark.

Earlier exploration rounds and alternative Signal variants are historical design concepts only. They are not approved production artwork. The canonical production source is the SVG stored at `official/goreecloud-logo.svg`.

## Identity Meaning

The logo is an abstract GoreeCloud mark rather than a literal illustration of a cloud, server, application, network, house, shield, or other implementation-specific object.

Its visual meaning is:

- **Outer form:** my personally controlled digital environment and the GoreeCloud space that belongs to me.
- **Open boundary:** independence without isolation. GoreeCloud remains capable of interacting with the wider digital world without surrendering ownership or control.
- **Central core:** the individual and family remain at the center of GoreeCloud rather than a provider, application, or infrastructure component.
- **Outward path:** intentional connection to external systems and the wider digital world on my terms.
- **Unified geometry:** ownership, privacy, independence, continuity, and controlled connection are treated as parts of one coherent environment.

The logo therefore represents what GoreeCloud is about rather than the particular platforms, applications, servers, or technologies used to implement it.

## Canonical Geometry

The canonical mark is defined by the vector paths in `official/goreecloud-logo.svg`. The SVG is the authoritative geometric source.

Raster exports, favicons, social-profile images, documentation artwork, repository artwork, and other derivatives must preserve the same underlying identity. Platform-specific adaptations may adjust rasterization, padding, masking, or presentation when required, but must not redraw or reinterpret the core mark without approval.

## Usage Boundary

I use this logo to identify the GoreeCloud ecosystem and platform as a whole.

I do not use the GoreeCloud platform logo as the generic default icon for every GoreeCloud application or service. Individual applications and services retain their own unique canonical visual identities while sharing compatible GoreeCloud and Glaze UI visual DNA.

The logo may be used for appropriate GoreeCloud-wide surfaces such as platform identity, organization/repository identity, public branding, documentation, ecosystem-level presentation, social identity, and other contexts where GoreeCloud itself—not one individual product—is the subject.

## Production Requirements

All official derivatives must:

- originate from the canonical SVG;
- preserve the approved geometry;
- remain recognizable at their intended size;
- avoid stretching, skewing, arbitrary rotation, or structural alteration;
- avoid unapproved recoloring;
- retain adequate surrounding space and contrast;
- remain visually distinct from individual application and service icons;
- use vector artwork where practical and high-quality raster exports where raster formats are required.

## Historical Concepts

Files under `concepts/` are design-history records. They must not be treated as approved GoreeCloud branding merely because they remain in the repository.

The approved identity supersedes Round 1, Round 2, Round 3, Round 4, earlier Signal variations, and other rejected or superseded explorations.

## Continuing Work

The approved mark establishes the canonical GoreeCloud logo and blue identity direction. Supporting production assets may be added without changing the approved underlying mark, including monochrome and reversed variants, transparent-background exports, favicon sizes, social and repository assets, wordmark lockups, clear-space specifications, minimum-size guidance, and platform-specific raster derivatives.

Any material change to the underlying geometry, meaning, or approved color identity requires a new explicit approval decision and corresponding documentation update.

## Final Decision

I approve Unified Clean in blue as the official GoreeCloud platform logo. I maintain its canonical source in this repository so GoreeCloud identity can be reproduced consistently, validated, versioned, and carried across appropriate surfaces without depending on undocumented artwork or memory.
